About Trouvus

Closing the gap between AI possibility and organizational reality.

Trouvus integrates research, strategy, organizational design and implementation discipline to help leaders make consequential technology decisions.

Where research meets implementation.

Trouvus was founded by Dr. Mitt Nowshade Kabir to translate advances in AI, digital transformation and technology management into practical business-model, operating-model and workforce decisions.

The firm’s perspective spans management scholarship, entrepreneurship, executive education and applied transformation. This multidisciplinary foundation enables Trouvus to examine not only whether a technology works, but where it creates value, how it changes work and what organizational capabilities are required to sustain it.

Operating principles

Evidence before enthusiasm

We distinguish demonstrated capability, informed inference and unproven promise.

Strategy before technology

We start with the decision, value logic and operating context.

Capability before scale

Transformation becomes durable when ownership and competence exist inside the organization.

Human agency alongside automation

Critical judgment and adaptive capacity must remain load-bearing organizational assets.

Measurable outcomes over activity

Workshops, pilots and systems are means; business and organizational outcomes are the test.

Responsible transformation by design

Governance, risk and accountability should be built into the operating model.
