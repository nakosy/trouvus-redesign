Systems · Markets

MIOS Elite V

A deterministic market decision system. Verified market data in; disciplined regime, trigger, risk and action output out — and a refusal when the data cannot be verified.

Operating

What it is not

Not a trading bot. Not a recommendation engine.

Both of those exist, both are easier to build, and both fail in the same way: the output is a confident sentence whose derivation nobody can reconstruct.

MIOS is a doctrine-constrained command system. Doctrine — the rules governing regime, trigger and risk — is set before the run and is not renegotiated during it. The engine applies it. Language models sit alongside the chain to explain, summarise, audit and argue with the result. They are not permitted to produce the final label.

Autonomous order execution is explicitly out of scope. The chain ends at a human, by design and not by omission. A system that both decides and acts removes the one checkpoint that makes the decision defensible.

Behaviour

What it emits, and what it refuses.

The action-label set is closed. Eight labels are answers; the ninth is a refusal. Adding one requires a schema change, a contract test and a rendering test — it cannot happen at runtime.1

ConditionWhat the system does
Price verified, doctrine satisfiedEmits one of eight action labels, with the run record behind it.
Price cannot be verifiedEmits BLOCKED. No buy zone, stop, target, score or tier may be shown as authoritative beside it.
Mock or fallback data in the runMarked visibly as unverified. Cannot produce an actionable label at all.
Risk check failsThe action is downgraded or blocked. Never silently widened.
No label fits the engine outputEmits BLOCKED rather than approximating to the nearest label.
The refusal is the feature. Eight of nine labels are the easy part; every system has those. A system that cannot say I do not know will instead say something plausible, and plausible output is the kind that gets acted on before anyone checks.
Build

What it runs on.

AccountabilityAudit trail, run cards, full trace replay
OutputClosed action-label set, nine values, refusal included
Order executionOut of scope — the chain ends at a named human

Trace replay is the part worth pressing us on. Every emitted label can be reconstructed from the run record: what was retrieved, what passed each gate, and which rule produced the verdict. That is what makes an answer auditable rather than merely confident.

Standing statement

Decision support, not advice.

MIOS produces decision support. It does not place orders and does not constitute financial advice.

We publish no performance figures, returns, backtest results or user counts, and we will not produce them on request. A number without a reproducible derivation is exactly the kind of claim the rest of this site argues against, and we are not going to make an exception for our own product.
