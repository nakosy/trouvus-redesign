Results and evidence

Claims should be classified, documented and easy to evaluate.

Trouvus distinguishes verified engagements, anonymized client work, research evidence and illustrative scenarios. We do not present hypothetical outcomes as completed client results.

Verified client engagement

Approved, attributable evidence

Used when client identity, scope, intervention and outcomes have been documented and approved for publication.

Publication fields: client · challenge · mandate · intervention · deliverables · verified outcomes · client quotation.

Anonymized engagement

Real work, protected identity

Used when confidentiality prevents attribution but the work and outcomes can be documented.

Publication fields: sector · scale · operating context · mandate · intervention · evidence status.

Research and intellectual evidence

Frameworks, publications and programs

Demonstrates the intellectual basis for Trouvus methods through research, books, articles, executive education and original frameworks.

Illustrative scenario

A model—not a client claim

Shows how value could be created in a defined context. Every scenario is labelled at the top as hypothetical.

Permission

Classification
