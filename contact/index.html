Request a briefing.

A briefing is a conversation about one decision your organisation has to make — what would have to be true for AI to help with it, and what would have to be measured first. It is not a capability presentation, and there is nothing to sit through.

What to expect

Forty-five minutes, one decision.

Bring a process or a decision that carries consequences. We will tell you what we would need to measure before making any claim about it, and whether we think the answer is a system, a baseline, or nothing at all.

If we think you do not need us, we will say so in that call rather than in month three.

Before you write

Two things worth knowing.

Every engagement starts with measurement. Whatever the eventual system, the first month is a baseline: one process, measured rather than estimated. If that sounds like a delay, it is worth reading why we insist on it — the alternative is a business case whose inputs nobody can defend.

We publish one engagement, with its limits. Not a portfolio, not logos, not testimonials. If you want to know what we have actually done and what it did not establish, that page exists and says so plainly.
